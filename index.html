About Us

Education: Brighter Minds, Brighter Future

LEAF is an NGO founded by 21st-century academic and educational leaders. We believe education solves humanity's greatest challenges, making it our core mission to empower individuals for lasting societal change. Since our inception, we've focused on Agriculture, Education, Empowerment, and Environment (AEEE) to address critical needs in underserved communities, fostering student development and sustainable practices.
